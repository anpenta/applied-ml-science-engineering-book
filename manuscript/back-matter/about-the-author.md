# About the author

Andreas Pentaliotis is a machine learning engineer who bridges science and engineering to build scalable machine learning systems. He has worked across the full lifecycle of machine learning projects, from research to production. He also holds a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Groningen, where his research led to the publication of a novel reinforcement learning algorithm.

Alongside his professional work, he develops open-source software and writes technical books. He wrote this book to share his perspective on the scientific and engineering decisions involved in applied machine learning, drawing on his professional experience and relevant technical literature.
