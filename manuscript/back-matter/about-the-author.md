# About the author

Andreas Pentaliotis is a machine learning engineer who bridges science and engineering to build scalable machine learning systems. He has worked across the full lifecycle of machine learning projects, from research to production. He also holds a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Groningen, where his research led to the [publication of a novel reinforcement learning algorithm](https://www.scitepress.org/PublicationsDetail.aspx?ID=1RfAEflz3Gk=&t=1).

Alongside his professional work, he wrote this book to bring together his positions on the technical decisions involved in applied machine learning. He also built [mldebug](https://github.com/anpenta/mldebug), a lightweight Python package for validating and comparing datasets in machine learning pipelines.
